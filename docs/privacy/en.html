Privacy Policy — 1-Bit Dice

Effective: May 29, 2026 · Last updated: May 29, 2026

1. Who we are

The 1-Bit Dice app is developed by AM2 Studios. Privacy contact: am2.studios.contact@gmail.com.

2. Summary

1-Bit Dice does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information used by the app stays exclusively on your device.

3. Data stored locally on your device

The app saves only your preferences and content you create, always in your device's local storage (no server sync):

  • Selected color palette
  • Selected language
  • Sound and haptic feedback preferences
  • Dice animation style and speed
  • Last selected dice type and quantity
  • Roll history
  • Custom game presets you create

You can erase this information at any time by uninstalling the app, clearing the history inside the app, or using your operating system's "clear data" option.

4. Personal data

The app does not request, receive, or store your name, email, phone number, address, location, photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or any other personal or sensitive data.

5. Third-party services

1-Bit Dice does not use analytics, advertising, social networks, external login, crash reporting, or any other SDK that sends data off-device.

6. Permissions

The app does not request sensitive system permissions (camera, location, contacts, microphone, etc.). The only system API interaction is haptic feedback (a short vibration on the roll button) when enabled by the user in Settings.

7. Children

The app's content is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data, the app complies with children's data protection laws (including COPPA in the US and LGPD in Brazil).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be published at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will be flagged inside the app in the following release.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: am2.studios.contact@gmail.com.
